Privacy Policy

Zimko
Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what the Android game Zimko ("the app") does with information. The developer is avtech media ("we", "us"). Contact: mythologyhunt.og@gmail.com.

Who This App Is For

Zimko is a racing game for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Information We Store on Your Device

The app does not ask you to create an account, and it does not send your game progress to us.

Progress is saved only on your phone, in the app's local storage. That save can include coins, unlocked cars, installed parts, upgrades, lives, the current level, and your best result. The save is encrypted on the device. Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app, deletes it. We cannot restore it, because we never receive a copy.

Advertising

The app shows ads with Google AdMob. Ads can appear when a race ends, when you choose to watch an ad for a reward such as extra lives or doubled coins, and in similar game-over moments.

We do not receive your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location from these ads. Google may collect and use:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address and general device information (such as device model, language, and Android version)
  • Ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped

Google uses this to deliver, measure, and limit ads, and may use it for personalized advertising unless you opt out.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Ad partners are described at: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android settings under Google, then Ads. The app needs an internet connection only to load these ads.

Permissions

The app asks for internet access and network-state access so ads can load. The AdMob library also uses the advertising ID.

The app does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage outside its own app data.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not run our own analytics or accounts. The only third party that receives information through the app is Google, as described above, when an ad is requested.

Retention

Game progress stays on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall. Ad data is kept by Google under Google's own retention rules. We do not keep a server copy of your play.

Your Choices

  • Delete local progress by clearing Zimko's storage in Android app settings, or by uninstalling.
  • Limit ad personalization from Android's Ads settings.
  • Email mythologyhunt.og@gmail.com with a privacy question. Because we do not hold an account for you, we usually cannot identify a specific player on a server.
